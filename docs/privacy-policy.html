Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

BTC Seed Recovery ("the App") is developed and maintained by the developer ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

BTC Seed Recovery is a fully offline tool for recovering your own Bitcoin seed phrases and managing your own wallets. We do not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit your seed phrases, private keys, or wallet data to any server.
  • Create user accounts or require any registration.
  • Use analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising identifiers.
  • Share any data with third parties.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data the App stores remains exclusively on your device:

  • Encrypted wallet keys – your seed phrase or private key, encrypted with AES-256 using a PIN you choose, stored in Android's encrypted SharedPreferences. We never see this data.
  • App preferences – theme, language, and UI settings stored locally.
  • Session statistics – recovery attempt counts stored locally for your reference.

3. Internet Access

The App connects to the Internet only when you explicitly request it, for the following read-only operations:

  • Querying the public mempool.space API to display the balance of your Bitcoin addresses.
  • Querying the same API to display your transaction history.
  • Broadcasting a signed transaction you choose to send.

Your private keys and seed phrases are never sent over the network. Only Bitcoin addresses (public information recorded on the blockchain) are transmitted to check balances.

4. Biometric Authentication

The App optionally uses Android's Biometric API (fingerprint or face recognition) to protect access to your wallet. Biometric data is processed entirely by the Android operating system and is never accessible to or stored by the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the updated policy at this URL.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
