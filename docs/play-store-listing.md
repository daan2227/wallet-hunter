## Full description (4000 chars max)

**Lost some words of your Bitcoin seed phrase? BTC Seed Recovery helps you get back into your own wallet.**

If you wrote down your 12 or 24-word seed phrase and can no longer read 1 to 4 words, this app systematically tries every possible BIP39 combination until it finds the valid phrase that matches your Bitcoin address.

### How it works

1. Enter your partial seed phrase — mark missing words with `???`
2. Optionally enter a Bitcoin address you know belongs to your wallet
3. Tap Recover — the app tests every valid combination
4. Once found, import the complete seed directly into the built-in wallet

### What you can do with the built-in wallet

- View your Bitcoin balance (P2PKH, P2SH, Bech32, Taproot)
- See full transaction history
- Send Bitcoin to any address
- Generate a QR code to receive funds
- Import by seed phrase or private key (WIF)
- Watch-only mode for monitoring addresses

### Security

- Your seed phrase is encrypted with AES-256 using a PIN you set
- Fingerprint / face unlock for fast access
- No account required — no data leaves your device
- Internet is used only to check balances on the public Bitcoin blockchain (mempool.space)
- Private keys and seed phrases are NEVER transmitted over the network

### Privacy

BTC Seed Recovery is a fully offline tool. We collect zero personal data, use no analytics, and have no advertising. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
